What FlowOps records about you

Please read this before you join. It describes what this product keeps about your work, who can see
it, and what happens if you leave.

What is kept

Your name, your email address, the role you hold, and who you report to.

Everything you do here that changes something is recorded: the task you accepted, the deadline you
proposed, the moment you marked something complete, the question you asked. Each record names who
acted and when, and once written it is never altered or deleted. That record is how the product can
answer what happened months later without anyone having to remember.

Who can see it

The people above you in the reporting line, and whoever the workspace owner has given access. If you
are moved to report to somebody else, they can see your work from that moment, and the person you
reported to before cannot.

How you are measured, and how you are not

FlowOps compares your work against your own history and against reference figures. Those figures are
reference points, never verdicts.

You are compared against colleagues only when at least three people are in the comparison. Below
that, the product refuses to show the comparison rather than showing a weaker version of it — because
in a group of two, a comparison is a statement about one named person.

No decision about you is made by software. The product surfaces what happened; a person decides
what it means.

If you leave

Your name, your email address and your password are destroyed. The work you did stays, in sequence,
attributed to Former member — because removing it would leave holes in records other people rely
on, and those records are about the work rather than about you.

This agreement is kept after you leave, with your identity removed from it. It stays because the fact
that consent was given, and when, is the thing it exists to record.

Agreeing

Agreeing to this is how you join. If you would rather not, decline the invitation: nothing is created
and nothing about you is kept beyond the fact that an invitation to your address was declined, and
when.
